Yashish Khurana — Strategy Portfolio

Strategies designed, run and post-mortemed with real capital at Lucidly Labs, 2024–2026 · rebuilt from scratch so the control logic and backtests can be read and rerun · github.com/0xYashish · @bullishbeers

This is a record of DeFi yield and structured-product work: leveraged stablecoin loops, delta-neutral funding carry, LP-and-hedge market making, supply-side lending clusters and coin-denominated carry vaults, executed from merkle-verified vaults on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and HyperEVM. The production code is Lucidly-owned, so every strategy here is a clean-room rewrite: the allocation and risk logic as pure Python, a replay or simulation on bundled data, and a static page generated from its results.json.

The transferable parts are the cost-aware control design (hysteresis, deadbands, churn accounting), the position and hedge maths, and the habit of writing down what broke. It is not mid-frequency alpha research; that work is in the quant-research repo, in progress. Where a result rests on synthetic data the page says so, and no result here should be read as a return claim.

What is common to all of them

  • Gate on the right metric. Levered net L·y − (L−1)·b, not the raw spread; window means, not single prints; hysteresis on every exit.
  • Split de-risking by cause and speed. A rate problem de-levers and holds the yield asset; a principal problem (markdown, depeg) exits regardless of carry; LTV safety runs on the fastest loop.
  • Same-block risk is bounded by sizing, not reaction. Markdown-to-liquidation at 8x is 4.37%; a junior tranche is 3–4× levered on its underlying's depeg; a 0.8 × liq guard at 3x is a 5% rally; a −35% gap liquidates a 0.65-LTV ladder. Leverage is chosen against those numbers.
  • Churn is the hidden cost. Entry fees × leverage, round-trip taker fees and basis, band-rotation IL + slippage: in every backtest here the deadband or sustain parameter moves the result more than the signal does.
  • Operations are part of the strategy. Preflight validators against the live merkle root, dry-run-by-default CLIs, emergency exits, heartbeats and paging; the incidents that bit (a drained gas wallet, a decoder missing a selector, orphaned async requests, a redemption into a term trough) each became an invariant.
